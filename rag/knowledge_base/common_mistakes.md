# Common Mathematical Mistakes & Pitfalls

## Algebra Mistakes
- WRONG: (a+b)^2 = a^2 + b^2. CORRECT: (a+b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2
- WRONG: sqrt(a^2 + b^2) = a + b. Square root does not distribute over addition.
- WRONG: log(a+b) = log(a) + log(b). Log of sum is NOT sum of logs.
- WRONG: 1/(a+b) = 1/a + 1/b. Reciprocal does not distribute.
- WRONG: Canceling terms in fractions that are added, not multiplied.

## Calculus Mistakes
- WRONG: d/dx[f*g] = f'*g'. Must use product rule.
- WRONG: Applying L'Hopital when limit is not indeterminate (0/0 or inf/inf).
- Forgetting the chain rule when differentiating composite functions.
- WRONG: integral of 1/x is ln(x). CORRECT: integral of 1/x is ln|x| + C.
- Forgetting +C in indefinite integrals.
- Not checking if function is continuous on the interval before applying theorems.

## Probability Mistakes
- Confusing permutations and combinations (order matters vs doesn't).
- Forgetting to consider "with replacement" vs "without replacement".
- WRONG: P(A and B) = P(A)*P(B) when events are NOT independent.
- Not using complementary counting when it's simpler: P(at least 1) = 1 - P(none).

## Linear Algebra Mistakes
- Assuming matrix multiplication is commutative.
- WRONG: det(A+B) = det(A) + det(B). Determinant is NOT linear.
- Forgetting that row operations change the determinant value.
- Confusing rank deficiency with zero determinant only for square matrices.

## General Problem-Solving Mistakes
- Not reading the question carefully (solving for the wrong variable).
- Ignoring domain restrictions (dividing by zero, negative under square root).
- Not verifying answers by substituting back into original equation.
- Ignoring units or dimensional analysis.
- Off-by-one errors in counting problems.
